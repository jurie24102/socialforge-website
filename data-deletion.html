Delete your data

Updated 24 October 2026

SocialForge’s current desktop build stores its workspace on your Mac. The developer does not hold a central copy of your drafts, videos, or platform credentials.

Remove a connection

  1. Wait for any upload to finish, then open SocialForge → Connections.
  2. Disconnect the selected account. This removes its saved account record and encrypted grant from the app.
  3. Revoke SocialForge or your own developer app in the relevant TikTok, Facebook, or Instagram account settings to remove the platform-side authorization.

Disconnecting does not erase earlier activity, exported files, the imported TikTok app configuration, or content already sent to a platform.

Remove the whole local workspace

  1. Quit SocialForge and back up anything you want to keep.
  2. In Finder, choose Go → Go to Folder and open ~/Library/Application Support/.
  3. Locate the SocialForge user-data folder (normally SocialForge or socialforge, depending on the build). Verify the folder belongs to SocialForge before removing it. It contains studio.json and, if connections were saved, credentials.json. Developer builds may use a custom folder.
  4. Move that app folder to Trash. Emptying Trash permanently removes it from normal local recovery; only do so when you are sure.
  5. Separately remove exported folders, private app-configuration JSON files you imported, and any backups or cloud copies you no longer need. Your original videos are not deleted automatically.

Remove uploaded or published content

Open the destination platform and delete the upload, draft, or post there. Removing local app data does not instruct TikTok or Meta to delete content they received.

Contact the developer

To request deletion of support correspondence or ask for help locating local data, email jurie24102@gmail.com with the subject “SocialForge data deletion”. Do not include passwords, API keys, access tokens, or client secrets. Requests concerning data held by a social platform should also be made to that platform.
