Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026 · Personal desktop build v0.1

SocialForge is a desktop video publishing application developed and operated by Jurie Wagener. This policy describes the current desktop app and this informational website. Contact jurie24102@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.

Information used by the desktop app

  • Drafts and media: draft titles, captions, selected destinations, planned dates, local video paths and technical media details, and any hosted video URL you supply.
  • Connected accounts: platform account identifiers, display names, connection dates, API version, and the authorization credentials needed for the connection.
  • Activity: upload and publishing status, timestamps, destination names, remote publishing identifiers, error summaries, and export folder paths. Export records may retain captions.

SocialForge uses this information to save your work, identify the destination you authorized, export or send selected media, check operation status, and display activity. The TikTok connector requests user.info.basic for account identity and video.upload for inbox uploads. It currently reads the account’s open ID and display name. It does not request TikTok direct publishing or private-message access.

Local storage and security

Drafts, account metadata, and activity are stored in the app’s user-data folder on your computer. Tokens, TikTok refresh grants, and an imported TikTok client configuration are encrypted through Electron safeStorage using macOS secure storage. Drafts and activity are not encrypted by SocialForge. Local videos are referenced in place; an export makes a copy in the folder you choose.

The current desktop app has no developer-operated publishing backend, advertising SDK, or analytics service. It does not send your drafts or credentials to the developer. Device access, operating-system backups, and any cloud-synced folders you use can affect access to local data.

When information leaves your computer

When you connect an account, SocialForge sends authorization requests and credentials to the matching platform to verify identity and obtain or refresh access. When you explicitly start a TikTok inbox upload, the selected video is transferred to TikTok. The inbox API does not receive your caption; you copy it separately to finish the post in TikTok. When you publish to Facebook or Instagram, the chosen platform receives the media or hosted URL, caption, account identifiers, and other data needed to process your request.

Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, your chosen media host, and your browser operate under their own policies. Their processing may take place in other countries. Disconnecting SocialForge does not delete content already sent to them. Review TikTok’s Privacy Policy and Meta’s Privacy Policy.

Website and support

This website does not contain an account signup form, advertising scripts, or analytics added by SocialForge. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection data, including IP addresses and request logs, to serve and protect the website. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement. If you email support, your email address and message are processed by the developer and email provider to answer the request. Do not send access tokens, client secrets, passwords, or private videos in support email.

Retention, removal, and your choices

Local app data remains until you remove it. Delete drafts in the app and disconnect accounts in Connections to remove their saved grants. Disconnecting does not revoke the grant at the platform, remove your imported developer-app configuration, or erase past activity. To erase all local app records and configuration, quit SocialForge and remove its user-data folder. Exports, source videos, backups, and published posts must be removed separately. See the data deletion instructions.

Support messages are retained while needed to resolve the request and maintain necessary correspondence. You may ask for access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent to the developer at the contact address above. The developer cannot remotely access or erase app data stored only on your device. SocialForge does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Eligibility and changes

SocialForge is intended for people eligible to use their chosen social platforms and is not directed to children. This policy will be updated if the app’s data practices change; the effective date identifies the current version.
